Our Story

A Symbol of Trusted Dental Care in Mathbaria

Oro Max Dental Care was founded with a simple mission — to bring safe, modern and world-class dental treatment to the people of Mathbaria and the surrounding areas of Pirojpur. We believe everyone deserves a healthy, confident smile.

With advanced equipment, experienced dentists and a genuinely caring approach, we have become a name families trust. From routine checkups to advanced procedures, we are committed to making every visit comfortable, affordable and reassuring.

  • Modern, well-equipped dental clinic
  • Experienced and qualified dental team
  • Strict hygiene and sterilization standards
  • Friendly, patient-first care for all ages
