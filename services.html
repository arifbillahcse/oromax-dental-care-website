What We Offer

A Full Range of Dental Treatments

From routine prevention to advanced restorative and cosmetic care, Oro Max Dental Care provides all the dental services you and your family need — with experienced dentists, modern equipment and a caring approach.

🦷

Dental Checkup & Consultation

A comprehensive oral examination covering your teeth, gums, jaw and bite. Early detection prevents small problems from becoming serious. We also provide personalised advice on brushing, diet and maintaining long-term oral health.

Preventive All Ages X-Ray Available
🪥

Scaling & Polishing

Professional ultrasonic cleaning to remove hardened plaque (tartar) and stubborn stains that regular brushing can't reach. Recommended every 6 months to prevent gum disease, tooth decay and bad breath.

Preventive Gum Health Fresh Breath
🪡

Tooth Filling

We restore decayed or chipped teeth with tooth-coloured composite fillings that blend naturally with your smile. The procedure is painless under local anaesthesia and stops decay from spreading further into the tooth.

Restorative Tooth-Coloured Same Visit
🩹

Tooth Extraction

Safe, controlled removal of severely damaged, infected or impacted teeth. We use local anaesthesia to ensure you feel no pain. After extraction we provide clear aftercare instructions and, where needed, replacement options such as implants or bridges.

Surgical Painless Wisdom Teeth
⚙️

Root Canal Treatment

When infection reaches the inner pulp of a tooth, root canal therapy saves the tooth by removing the infected tissue, cleaning the root canals and sealing them. Modern techniques make the procedure comfortable and often completed in one or two visits.

Save Your Tooth Pain Relief Endodontics
👑

Crown & Bridge

A dental crown caps a weakened or broken tooth to restore its full shape, strength and appearance. A bridge fills the gap left by one or more missing teeth by anchoring to the neighbouring teeth. Both are custom-made to match your natural shade.

Restorative Prosthetics Natural Look
🦴

Dental Implants

The gold standard for replacing missing teeth. A titanium post is placed in the jawbone to act as an artificial root, topped with a custom crown. Implants look, feel and function like natural teeth and can last a lifetime with proper care.

Permanent Bone Preservation Lifetime Solution
😁

Braces & Orthodontics

We straighten crooked teeth and correct misaligned bites using metal braces or clear aligners. Beyond aesthetics, well-aligned teeth are easier to clean and reduce the risk of decay, gum disease and jaw problems. Suitable for teenagers and adults.

Orthodontics All Ages Clear Aligners
✨

Teeth Whitening

Professional in-clinic whitening that safely lightens teeth by several shades in a single session. We use clinically tested bleaching agents for effective, long-lasting results — far superior to over-the-counter kits, with no damage to enamel.

Cosmetic Same-Day Results Enamel Safe
🧒

Kids Dentistry

Child-friendly dental care in a warm, welcoming environment. We focus on prevention — fluoride treatments, fissure sealants and early habit guidance — while building positive dental experiences that encourage healthy habits for life.

Paediatric Fluoride Fissure Sealants
